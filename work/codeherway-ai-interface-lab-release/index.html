Green CI wasn’t the release

AI Interface Lab passed its automated checks. That answered whether the course source met our checks, not whether a signed-in learner could open the published lessons. I treated those as separate questions and asked for separate evidence.

  • One course in the scoped publish
  • Five modules · 16 lessons

Evidence Snapshot

Publisher receipt

The scoped run reported a production publish from curriculum revision d681c0ad: one course, five modules, 16 lessons, and 48 lesson quiz questions. The final assessment was outside that run.

Source trail

The curriculum and platform registration work were reviewed separately. Their pull requests and the raw publish receipt are private; this page is the public-safe summary of the reported checkpoint.

Evidence boundary

A publish receipt reports what the job wrote. It is not an independent readback of published rows or a signed-in walkthrough against the intended deployment.

Problem and Users

Learners need catalog material to open and work. Curriculum and platform are maintained separately, so valid, merged source can still differ from what the platform serves.

Key Product Decisions

Publish only AI Interface Lab for this release rather than republishing the full catalog. Track content validation, publication, and learner experience as separate milestones.

Engineering Decisions

Tie verification to source revision, target environment, and course scope. Use the publisher’s record counts to check what it reported writing. Reserve a learner-ready claim for independent readback and a signed-in walkthrough.

Accessibility Considerations

Automated source checks and a publish receipt do not verify keyboard or screen-reader use in the served lesson. Include those checks in the learner walkthrough.

Performance Considerations

The scoped publish did not measure lesson loading performance on the intended deployment. That requires a separate deployed-page check.

Current Status and Limits

The scoped run reported publication. At that checkpoint, published rows had not been independently confirmed, and the course had not been walked as an authenticated learner on desktop and mobile. No learner outcomes are claimed.

What I Would Improve Next

Record a signed-in walkthrough of a lesson and its quiz on desktop and mobile, tied to the deployed revision. Track the final assessment separately so it cannot inherit a pass from a job that did not publish it.
